The atelier

Fewer things,
better made.

We started Vesper in a two-room studio in Alfama with one rule: make clothes we'd still want to wear in ten years. No trend cycles, no seasonal churn — just a slow, deliberate release of pieces we've tested to death.

Every garment is cut and sewn within 90 kilometres of Porto, in a family-run factory we visit every month. We know the names of the people who make your clothes. That's not marketing — it's just how we prefer to work.
